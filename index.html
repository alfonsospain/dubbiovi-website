Built for Academic and Professional Workflows

Powerful tools that simplify translation, speech synthesis, and dubbing projects.

Local-First Studio

Transcription, voice synthesis, and timeline projects run directly on your workstation. Local-first storage guarantees local data control, offline capability, and native .dubbiovi project files.

Whisper Transcription

Run speech recognition locally with downloadable Whisper runtimes and models for private offline execution, or connect optional cloud transcription services for additional flexibility.

Local & Cloud TTS

Generate speech with local Piper neural voices for offline synthesis, or use optional cloud providers like Google Gemini and OpenAI-compatible endpoints. Create, regenerate, and audition audio takes with ease.

Timeline & Mixer

Audio waveform timeline, dedicated Playback Mixer, and interactive Dub Preview. Synchronize speech segments, adjust volume levels, and fit voice timing with visual feedback.

Video & Audio Export

Render synchronized dubs directly to final video files, export individual speech takes, mixed audio, and structured bilingual script tables (Word, Excel, CSV, JSON).

Open & Provider-Agnostic

Free and open under EUPL-1.2. Designed for macOS and Windows with support for offline local runtimes as well as optional cloud AI providers.

Process

The DubbiOvi 2.0 Translation Workflow

A streamlined, 5-step process tailored for professional audiovisual translators. Click the steps to view details.

Step 1

Import Video & Media

Load standard video media containers (MP4, MKV, AVI, WebM) directly from your local filesystem. DubbiOvi 2.0 extracts audio tracks offline, initializes project timeline waveforms, and manages native .dubbiovi project files.

Step 2

Whisper Audio Transcription

Transcribe dialog from your media using local Whisper models for offline processing or connect optional cloud transcription services, generating time-aligned segments automatically.

Step 3

Context-Aware Translation

Translate and adapt dialog with optional AI assistance or manual editing, tailoring phrasing to character rhythm, target language constraints, and scene context.

Step 4

Voice Synthesis & Dub Preview

Synthesize voice takes using local Piper neural models or optional cloud providers. Fine-tune timing, fitting, volume and alignment against the timeline, and audition sync with Dub Preview.

Step 5

Final Video & Audio Export

Render synchronized dubs directly into completed video files, export individual speech takes, mixed audio, or generate formatted Word scripts, Excel data sheets, and standard SRT/VTT subtitles.

About the DubbiOvi 2.0 Project

DubbiOvi 2.0 was created and developed by Alfonso C. Rodríguez Fernández-Peña.

The software serves as a modern platform for teaching audiovisual translation techniques, facilitating speech-processing research datasets, and transferring AI dubbing technologies directly into educational and professional workflows. By enabling local control over proprietary keys, local models, and offline execution, the project aims to foster open translation studies globally.
